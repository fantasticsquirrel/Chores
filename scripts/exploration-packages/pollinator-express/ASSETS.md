# Pollinator Express artifact attribution

The downloadable package contains no downloaded image, texture, audio or font
asset. Its garden, flowers, enlarged pollen structures, bee, nest, route and
reach marks are authored procedural geometry in this repository.

Institutional sources listed in the registry and
`docs/pollinator-express-copy-research.md` support lesson copy only. They are
not visual assets, and they do not support the invented coordinates, reach
values, route, counts or habitat effect.
