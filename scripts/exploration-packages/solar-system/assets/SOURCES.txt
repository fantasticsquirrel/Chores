# Space exhibit assets and data

Verified 2026-11-01.

- `nasa-blue-marble-2k.jpg`: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation, August 2004 cloud-free land/ocean color map. Source https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/ links to https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/august/world.200408.3x5400x2700.jpg . Resized from 5400×2700 to 2048×1024 and JPEG encoded for offline use. This is not today's Earth imagery. Credit NASA Earth Observatory.
- NASA media policy: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ . NASA imagery generally is not subject to US copyright and may be used in educational simulations with attribution; no NASA endorsement is implied. No third-party copyright marking appears on this asset's source.
- Other planetary globe textures, satellite geometry, stars and laboratory apparatus are authored procedural illustrations, not observational maps. Texture details do not encode measurements; gas and ice giant banding is illustrative and does not imply a solid surface.
- Eight-planet order and rounded mean-distance, orbital-period and equatorial-diameter values: NASA Planetary Fact Sheet, https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/ , and NASA's Basics of Space Flight, https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter1-2/ . Planets move according to their stored period, not equal speeds; positions are illustrative, circular and coplanar rather than a live ephemeris.
- Astronomical-unit definition and scale context: NASA Cosmic Distances, https://science.nasa.gov/universe/cosmic-distances/ . One AU is about Earth's mean distance from the Sun, roughly 150 million kilometres.
- Separate size and distance scales: NASA/JPL Make a Scale Solar System, https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/edu/resources/project/make-a-scale-solar-system/ . The orbit mission compresses unequal mean distances and enlarges planets; the diameter bay instead uses one linear planet-diameter scale and omits the Sun and orbit gaps.
- No remote assets or child identifiers are requested by the exhibits. All texture bytes ship with the app.
