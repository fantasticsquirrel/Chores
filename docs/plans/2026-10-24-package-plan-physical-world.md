# Physical-world package conversion plan (2026-10-24)

## Architecture and acceptance

Convert nine current expansion Explorations into independent `worker-rich-v1` packages. Every package owns its scene geometry, procedural assets, deterministic model, control transitions, guided predictions and explanations. No scene-name dispatch into bundled app implementations. Copy and adapt the existing authored scenes rather than replace rich rendering with generic diagrams. The trusted renderer accepts bounded serialized geometry/material/camera data; package code runs only in the opaque worker sandbox. Generic renderer/schema capabilities are coordinated separately.

Entrypoints: `scripts/exploration-packages/<original-id>/worker.ts`; published IDs append `-package`. Original native bundled Explorations remain intact. Package support is explicitly web-only until native package execution exists. No push or deployment is part of this authoring step.

## Per-topic implementation and editorial review

1. **Machine Workshop (`machine-workshop`)**: Preserve articulated pulley, lever and gear winch, routed rope, crate and toothed gears. Controls select machine, advantage 1–4, load 10–100 kg and lift 0–1 m; retain marker picking/drag and equivalent slider. Package model preserves F=mg/ratio, effort distance=lift×ratio, work conservation, crank turns and torque. Guided trials compare one/two supporting sections and 12/48 driven teeth at equal load/lift. Test zero lift (nonzero balancing force, zero work), gear ratio extremes and resets. Explain ideal slow lifting without friction, stretch or machine mass; not equipment design.
2. **Heat House (`heat-house`)**: Preserve house cutaway, insulation layers, windows, sample, directional heat arrows and warm point light. Controls wall material, added insulation 0–20 cm, windows 2–18 m², indoor/outdoor temperature, thickness drag. Model Q=UAΔT with fixed 60 m² envelope and U=1/(0.17+0.2/k+insulation/0.04). Predict whether wall insulation changes unchanged window transfer; compare window area replacing opaque wall. Test ΔT zero/reversed and materials/extremes. Preserve representative-conductivity, steady-temperature and omitted roof/drafts/sunlight caveats.
3. **Pressure Tank (`pressure-tank`)**: Preserve transparent narrow/wide/tapered vessels, water surface/outlet, jet trajectory and markers. Controls surface 0.2–3 m, outlet 0.1–2.5 m and shape; retain water-level drag. Model head=max(surface−outlet,0), pressure=ρgh, speed=√(2gh), range and shape volume. Compare surface rise at fixed outlet, then shape at equal head. Verify dry outlet/no jet, volume differences with unchanged head/speed, and range maximum. Retain static gauge pressure versus atmospheric free jet and instantaneous-not-draining distinction.
4. **Moving Plates (`moving-plates`)**: Preserve procedural vertex-colored terrain cutaways, ridge, subducting crust and transform-road strain/offset. Boundary select resets; progress/step/pick and boundary reset retain semantics. Compare Apart/Together at 40% diagram progress; four transform loading steps followed by slip on fifth. Test threshold 60, residual 12 and capped progression. Preserve mostly solid mantle, oceanic–continental-specific collision, all-boundary earthquake possibility and invented nonforecast timing.
5. **Cloud and Rain Lab (`cloud-rain-lab`)**: Preserve terrain, highlighted air parcel, cloud droplet cluster and rain drops. Controls temperature 20–35°C, vapor 2–14 g/kg, lift 0–2000 m, growth 0–100; parcel pick adds 200 m. Model approximate condensation level and 9.8/6°C per km cooling. Compare identical parcel at 600/1800 m; compare cloud growth 30/90 at fixed temperature/vapor/lift. Verify clear/cloud/rain states and that growth alone cannot form cloud. Keep fixed 1000 hPa, no ice/mixing, growth threshold not droplet size/rain forecast.
6. **Magnetic Fields (`magnetic-fields`)**: Preserve bar poles, field curves, compass array, large probe and force arrows. Gap, flip, probe x/z, pole picks and compass picks retain behavior. Model combined softened exterior field vectors and cancellation. Compare flipping at fixed gap/probe; compare N–N midpoint and z=2. Verify zero-field no preferred direction and excluded inside-bar domain. Preserve diagram-unit/non-tesla, no-isolated-poles and omitted Earth field caveats.
7. **Cell Explorer (`cell-explorer`)**: Preserve plant/animal cutaways, enlarged selectable organelles, translucent shell, lipid membrane, molecules/carrier. Controls type, selected part, molecule, carrier, concentrations and step/reset. Switching animal removes plant-only selection. Compare oxygen/glucose without carrier and glucose with/without carrier from outside 12/inside 4. Verify conserved total in equal volumes, gradient reversal and equilibrium; do not imply cell walls replace membranes. Keep passive-only, no reaction/active-transport and no real-time implications.
8. **Shape Slicer (`shape-slicer`)**: Preserve transparent rotated solids, cutting plane, ruler/pick arrows and simultaneous straight-on 2D cross-section. Controls solid, tilt/turn, offset with slider/key/drag, centered/diagonal presets. Exact polyhedral edge intersections and analytic sphere πr² stay package-owned. Compare central square/hexagonal cube slices and sphere cuts at 0/0.6. Verify point/line tangencies, empty cuts, tetrahedron sections and area. Camera changes view only; 2D equal-scale section is required, not expendable decoration.
9. **Adaptation Island (`adaptation-island`)**: Preserve rocky/sandy terrain and twenty beetle symbols approximating proportions, plus predation scene. Controls habitat/predation, initial share, one/five generations and reset. Model deterministic normalized expected frequencies with illustrative .9/.5 weights. Compare habitats after exactly five generations, and predation on/off from identical 50/50 state. Verify 0/100% absorbing boundaries, generation cap 20, initial-share reset and habitat affecting future only. Keep inheritance, no individual trait choice, no mutation/migration/drift and no field forecast distinctions.

## Generic capability requirements

- Bounded indexed triangle buffers with positions/normals/vertex colors/UVs; preserve original tubes, beveled extrusions, curved shells and terrain rather than silently substitute primitives.
- Node transforms/quaternions, material double-sided/depth-write/physical transmission, point lights, camera framing, label projection and generic picking.
- Mixed 3D plus bounded vector 2D output for the straight-on section. Pointer input must be bounded and data-only, with keyboard/slider equivalents. No DOM/shader/network/callback bridge.
- Real tree-shaken Three author construction serializes into generic data; source budget 1 MiB and response budget 4 MiB under explicit rich runtime. Measure actual budgets before accepting each package.

## Verification gates

Characterize original state transitions and model outputs first. Test each package initial state, every control and preset, prediction/baseline/comparison sequence, reset, edge cases, finite output, node/material/triangle budgets and original-versus-package geometry count/bounds. Render desktop and 390px narrow screenshots for defaults and meaningful extremes; inspect readable evidence/units, controls and visual fidelity. Exercise pointer and keyboard paths. Test package-independent worker loading; no imports into bundled simulation runtime. Parser must reject malformed buffers, unknown keys and nonfinite values. Preserve heartbeat termination, CSP/network isolation, archive traversal rejection, immutable approval/version boundaries and authoritative backend permissions. Run adjacent frontend/shared/mobile tests and build/typecheck plus git diff --check. Record unchecked browser/editorial/source reviews honestly rather than label them passed.
