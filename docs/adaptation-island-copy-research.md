## Bounded teaching model

The fictional population has dark and pale inherited shell-color variants.
With visual predators, the ground-matching variant receives relative
survival-and-descendant weight 0.9 and the contrasting variant 0.5. Without
predators, both weights are 0.9. Weighted shares are normalized to 100% each
generation. These are invented deterministic teaching weights, not measured
survival probabilities or forecasts.

Two controlled investigations require a prediction before evidence:

1. Compare dark and pale ground from the same 50/50 start, predators, weights
   and five-generation duration. Expected dark share is 95.0% versus 5.0%.
2. Compare predation present and absent on dark ground while holding the 50/50
   start and five generations fixed. Unequal weights give 95.0% dark; equal
   weights keep the population 50/50.

Each path reports correct or incorrect feedback, changed and held variables,
exact expected percentages, causal explanation, stale-evidence clearing and a
full reset across web, native and standalone package.

## Model and display boundary

Twenty beetle symbols round shares to five-percentage-point increments; the
numeric percentage is the evidence. Survivors are assumed to contribute the
same number of descendants, the total is fixed, generations do not overlap and
shell color is treated as a directly copied variant without modeling genes or
mating. A variant at 0% cannot return because mutation and migration are absent.
The island layout and camouflage are schematic, not a field study.
